# competitor watch

read the saved pages in {WATCH} and tell me what the other lot changed since last
week.

## what i want

- pricing changes, with the old and the new number
- anything new on the front page
- anything that disappeared

## how to compare

the folder has one subfolder per week. compare the newest against the one
before it. if there is only one week, say so and stop.

ignore anything inside a script or style block, it is all noise.
